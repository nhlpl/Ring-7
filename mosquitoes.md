### 4. Host‑Seeking Sensory Suite: The Triangulation of Breath

**Formula 150: Mosquito Host‑Seeking Sensor Fusion (Bayesian)**
\[

\]
where \(i = \{ \text{CO}_2, \text{IR}, \text{octenol}, \text{lactic} \}\), and \(\alpha_i\) is the reliability weighting (learned from experience). Noise in the CO₂ channel (e.g., wind turbulence) automatically shifts weight to the thermal channel, and vice versa.

**Discovery:** The CO₂ receptor neurons on the maxillary palps exhibit a **derivative response** —they fire only when the concentration is increasing. This allows the mosquito to sense the direction of a breath plume, not just its presence. **Formula 151: CO₂ Derivative Detection Threshold**

\text{SNR}_{\text{CO}_2} = \frac{d[\text{CO}_2]/dt}{\sigma_{\text{ambient}}} \approx \frac{0.1\ \text{ppm/s}}{0.05\ \text{ppm}} = 2.0
