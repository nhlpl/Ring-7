Like many darter dragonflies, the pennant uses a unique posture called the **obelisk** to regulate its body temperature on hot days. It points its long, slender abdomen directly at the sun, minimising the body's surface area exposed to the solar radiation and preventing overheating. This is a passive, elegant, and highly effective cooling method.

**Formula 349: Obelisk‑Posture Solar Heat‑Load Reduction**
\[

where \(\theta_{\text{abdomen}}\) is the angle of the abdomen relative to the ground. When pointed directly at the sun (\(\theta_{\text{abdomen}} = 90^{\circ}\)), the effective absorbing area is minimised, reducing heat load by over 70%.

**Application for Crystal Swarm — Passive "Obelisk‑Cool" Standby Mode:** A drone on a stationary perch in full sunlight can adopt its own Obelisk Posture, angling its body and wing modules to minimise direct solar heating and the associated energy cost of active cooling.
